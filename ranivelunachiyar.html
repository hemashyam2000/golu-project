Rani Velu Nachiyar

Rani Velu Nachiyar affectionately called as Veeramangai was the first queen to form an all women army (Udayal Padai) to revenge against the Britishers who killed her husband. She successfully reclaimed Sivagangai along with her commander Kuyili, the first female suicide bomber who sacrificed herself and destroyed British entire weapons and ammunitions.
