Karaikkal Ammayar

Karaikkal Ammayar also known as Peyaar born as Punithavathi in Karaikal is a story of transition from a wealthy merchant's wife to a skeletal ascetic who won Lord Shiva's praise as "Mother". It is this great lady who first sang in the construct called padhikam and marked the last hymn of each padhikam with a signature. This has been followed by many later. Hence the padhikams sung by her are called mutha tirupadhikam (elder tirupadhikam).
